# ProgrammePulse: EXCO positioning and market expansion

Assessment date: 22 September 2026. Based on selected current working-tree code, repository evidence registers and vendor documentation. This is a strategic assessment, not a complete product audit or customer research. Vendor capabilities are advertised capabilities, not independently benchmarked results. Existing uncommitted contract and test changes were inspected selectively and left untouched.

## Recommendation

Position ProgrammePulse as an evidence-backed delivery decision service for services businesses using multiple operational systems. The executive outcome is an agreed decision on a material commitment, supported by reconciled evidence, an accountable owner and a follow-up date. Start with a paid, assisted review and productise the repeated workflow.

Make market configuration possible now; expand commercially one market at a time. Broad translation is premature without qualified demand. The best initial international opportunity may be a UK customer's overseas operation using English, rather than an entirely new language and sales channel.

Initial customer hypothesis: UK consultancies, agencies and technology services firms with roughly 50-500 delivery staff, multiple delivery/time/resource systems and recurring management reconciliation work. This range is a qualification hypothesis, not a measured market segment. PMO is the champion, COO/delivery director the likely buyer, CFO the financial reviewer and CTO the technical gatekeeper.

The defensible advantage would accumulate through reusable source mappings, versioned metric definitions, accepted reconciliations, reliable operations and customer outcomes. Code architecture and extra dashboards alone are easy to imitate. Customer data must remain portable; trust is a stronger retention mechanism than lock-in.

## What EXCO should receive

One concise review pack, scoped to the recipient's permissions, with a queue of decisions ordered by agreed materiality. Every item includes the commitment, what changed, evidence freshness and coverage, options, consequence, owner, decision deadline and follow-up outcome. Unknown impact remains unknown rather than becoming a fabricated financial estimate.

| Stakeholder | Decision to support | Evidence and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| CEO | Continue, stop or escalate a material commitment? | Strategic objective, milestone exposure, decision owner and outcome. Counts of overdue tasks are not a strategy assessment. |
| CFO | Which commercial exposure can we substantiate and act on? | Approved allocation, priced coverage, cost-to-date, contract basis and remaining-cost assumptions. Headroom is not forecast profit, revenue recognition or cash. |
| COO | Which demand or capacity mismatch needs intervention? | Same-period demand, bookings, working calendars and approved leave. A booking or low recorded utilisation does not prove available capacity. |
| CTO | Is the evidence dependable and the service supportable? | Source lineage, publication status, access boundaries, setup effort and operational evidence. Code controls do not prove a deployed security posture. |
| PMO | Can the pack be reproduced and actions closed with less effort? | Versioned pack, accepted exceptions, action history, correction time and total preparation/review effort. |

Priorities: explicit contract allocation; immutable reviewed packs; decisions/actions and outcome tracking; executive permissions; then bounded what-if scenarios using approved assumptions. Reuse existing reporting, snapshots and RAID models where their semantics fit. Avoid a parallel governance subsystem.

## Market, language and addressable market

Separate UI language, number/date format, reporting currency, contract currency, time zone, fiscal calendar, working calendar, legal entity and deployment region. A French-speaking user can work for a UK entity and review a USD contract. No setting should silently change another.

Recommended expansion order:

1. UK repeatability: three paid design partners as a proposed commercial gate, with at least two comparable review cycles each.
2. One overseas entity of an existing customer, or one English-speaking market with an accessible buyer. Ireland is a candidate to investigate, not an evidence-backed winner. Validate EUR, Europe/Dublin, local calendars, contracting and support requirements.
3. A second UI language only when named buyers fund the translation, terminology review and ongoing support burden. Complete Welsh where there is actual demand; do not advertise whole-product bilingual coverage prematurely.
4. Group consolidation after explicit entity permissions, allocation and approved FX policy are proven. Tenants remain security boundaries; subsidiaries are not automatically separate tenants or automatically mutually visible.

Translation expands theoretical reach. Reachable buyers, supported systems, procurement requirements and distribution determine the serviceable market. Calculate a bottom-up opportunity rather than quoting a generic global PPM market:

- TAM hypothesis: eligible organisations multiplied by a validated annual contract value, deduplicated where a group would buy centrally.
- SAM: the subset with supported workflows, geography, language, deployment and procurement needs.
- Obtainable revenue: qualified opportunities multiplied by observed win rate and annual value, constrained by implementation/support capacity and adjusted for renewal/churn over the chosen period.

Illustration only: 1,000 eligible accounts at GBP 12,000/year implies GBP 12m theoretical annual revenue; 100 reachable accounts implies GBP 1.2m SAM. Twenty qualified opportunities at a 20% win rate yield four customers and GBP 48,000 annual recurring value, before delivery cost. These are invented inputs, not a market estimate or recommended price. Keep one-off diagnostic revenue separate from ARR.

For each market, collect named-account counts, buyer interviews, procurement barriers, willingness to pay, connector prevalence, acquisition cost and service cost. Proceed only when conservative contribution from expected customers covers incremental localisation, legal, hosting, sales and support costs. Do not treat UI translation as market readiness. Data-transfer arrangements depend on actual processing and access: review the current [ICO international-transfer guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/) for the proposed operating model.

## Execution and evidence gates

Suggested sequence, not a delivery estimate:

- First 30 days: sell and deliver bounded diagnostics; measure the current workflow and assisted repeat; agree the actual EXCO decisions and metric definitions. Build the smallest evidence-pack slice alongside this learning.
- Next 30 days: allocation, decision lifecycle, permission-aware packs and market defaults; validate on customer records. Prioritise deployment assurance and connector support needed by those customers.
- Following 30 days: one international pilot, complete required localisation, verify calendars and currency behaviour, and measure support economics before adding another market.

Proposed pass criteria: three paid partners; two comparable cycles per partner; every material finding accepted or explicitly disputed; no unexplained differences in sampled reconciliations; no permission leaks; repeat preparation/review effort measured end to end; at least two buyers willing to renew at a price covering recurring delivery and support. These are proposed thresholds, not achieved results.

Competitive selling should lead with a reproducible before/after pack and customer-approved evidence. Keep scope and pricing tied to source complexity, portfolios and service effort rather than requiring every data contributor to buy a seat. Validate that packaging with buyers. Establish support ownership, recovery/restore evidence and the relevant security review before broadening deployment claims.
