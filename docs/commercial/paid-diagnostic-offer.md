# Paid weekly delivery evidence diagnostic

## Offer

Review one recurring delivery, programme or service report against one representative period of sanitised source exports. Return an evidence-led exception register, data limitations, decision actions and a measured recommendation to proceed, correct or stop.

This is an assisted professional service using the current product as an analysis workbench. It is not a promise of automatic onboarding, continuous integration, production hosting or savings.

## Intended buyer

A delivery director, PMO lead, COO delegate or head of operations who owns a recurring weekly review assembled from more than one source. Qualify the opportunity only when there is:

- a named recurring report and decision;
- a person accountable for preparing or approving it;
- measurable preparation or reconciliation effort;
- an operational consequence when the report is late, incomplete or wrong;
- authority to approve a small diagnostic or introduce its budget owner.

## Fixed scope

| Included | Excluded unless separately agreed |
|---|---|
| One reporting decision and representative period | Continuous sync or production deployment |
| Sanitised exports from agreed sources | API keys, unrestricted accounts or production write access |
| Definition and coverage review | Rebuilding the customer's operating model |
| Evidence-led findings with uncertainty | Automated individual performance scoring |
| One playback session and action register | Bespoke connector development |
| Baseline and delivery effort measurement | Guaranteed savings or operational outcomes |

## Deliverables

Handed over as one filled-in copy of
[`evidence-pack-template.md`](evidence-pack-template.md), which carries the
mandatory per-finding fields (scope, period, source record IDs, source and
extraction timestamps, definition version, numerator and denominator,
omissions, observed-vs-inferred, reviewer, action owner, disposition).
The exception register is generated by the Evidence Check (`/staffops/programme/evidence-check`, CSV export). The reviewer's judgement (confirming each finding, naming owners and recording decisions) stays human, but it is recorded in the product: record the check, decide each finding on the review page, and hand over its printable board pack and CSV register with owners and decisions filled in.

The customer's exports go into the product through
`/staffops/programme/import` (work items, then time entries; templates
downloadable there). Import problems such as unmapped statuses, unlinked
time, unknown billability and unmatched people are evidence gaps and belong
in the exception register. The delivery steps are in
[`first-sale-playbook.md`](first-sale-playbook.md).

1. Source and definition inventory with timestamps and known omissions.
2. Exception register linking each finding to evidence, coverage, owner and proposed decision.
3. Reconciliation summary that distinguishes observed facts, calculations and human judgments.
4. Baseline versus diagnostic preparation effort for the named weekly review.
5. Recommendation: stop, repeat by export, or scope a controlled connected pilot.

## Commercial structure to test

Do not publish a permanent price until delivery effort is measured. Quote a fixed diagnostic fee for the agreed scope and state assumptions. Record:

- fee invoiced and cash collected;
- founder, engineering and review hours;
- direct hosting or tooling cost;
- customer preparation hours before and during the diagnostic;
- requested work outside scope;
- purchase, repeat or stop decision.

The maximum economically rational fee is not automatically the calculated time saving. The buyer also considers implementation risk, existing licences, procurement effort and confidence in the result.

## Discovery conversation

1. Show me the last report and the decision it supported.
2. Who prepared, reconciled and approved it, and how long did each spend?
3. Which figures caused debate or manual checking?
4. What happened because information was missing, stale or wrong?
5. Which source is authoritative for each disputed figure?
6. What data could be provided as a sanitised export?
7. Who can approve a fixed-scope diagnostic?
8. What result would justify repeating or connecting it?

Avoid leading questions about desired features. Ask for the current artefact, process and cost.

## Qualification scorecard

Score each item 0, 1 or 2. Proceed to a proposal at 8 or more, provided a budget owner or credible introduction exists.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recurring decision | None | Irregular | Named and recurring |
| Reconciliation effort | Unknown/low | Some manual effort | Measured material effort |
| Consequence | None | Frustration | Delay, rework, margin or client risk |
| Multiple sources | One adequate source | Exports combined | Persistent cross-tool disagreement |
| Data access | Unavailable | Possible with effort | Sanitised representative export available |
| Ownership | No owner | User only | Owner and budget route identified |

## Success and stop rules

Success requires an accepted finding or a measured reduction in preparation effort, delivery at a viable internal cost, and a recorded repeat/purchase decision.

Stop or rescope when the existing source report already answers the decision adequately, exports cannot be supplied safely, definitions cannot be agreed, the buyer will not measure the baseline, or bespoke work would consume the likely contract value.
