## Evidence labels

- **Implemented:** code exists and relevant automated tests pass.
- **Fixture tested:** recorded or synthetic payloads exercise the contract.
- **Sandbox tested:** a disposable vendor account completed the acceptance checklist.
- **Pilot validated:** a customer reconciled known records and accepted the result.
- **Supported:** operational monitoring, credential lifecycle, provider change response and support ownership exist.
