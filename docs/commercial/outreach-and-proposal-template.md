# Outreach and diagnostic proposal templates

Use plain text and personalise the first sentence. Do not claim customer results that do not exist.

## Warm introduction request

> I am testing a short diagnostic for PMO and delivery leaders who spend time reconciling weekly delivery reviews across tools. It reviews one existing report and sanitised exports, then shows which conclusions have current evidence and which need attention. I am looking for someone who personally owns that reporting process, rather than general product feedback. Would you be comfortable introducing me to one relevant person?

## Direct message

> I am researching how PMO and delivery leaders assemble their weekly delivery review when work, capacity and time sit in different systems. I am not asking you to change tools or connect an account. Would you be willing to show me a sanitised version of the last report and explain where the manual checking happens? I can return a short assessment of the evidence and gaps. If there is a worthwhile diagnostic, I will scope and price it before requesting data or build work.

## Follow-up after discovery

> You described **[named reporting decision]**, prepared **[frequency]**, involving approximately **[people/hours]**. The main uncertainty was **[specific gap]**, and the operational consequence was **[consequence]**. I propose a fixed diagnostic using **[sanitised sources]** for **[period]**. It will return **[deliverables]** by **[date]** for **[fee]**. It excludes live connections and production deployment. At playback we will decide to stop, repeat by export, or scope a controlled pilot.

## Conversation evidence ledger

Record facts and exact buyer language where permitted. Separate observed facts, buyer opinions and your interpretation. A positive reaction, feature request or willingness to take another free call is not purchase evidence. Strong signals are access to the report owner, provision of a sanitised artefact, budget discussion, procurement action and payment.
