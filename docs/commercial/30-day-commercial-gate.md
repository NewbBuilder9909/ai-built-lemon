# 30-day commercial gate

This gate prevents product expansion from substituting for demand evidence.

## Week 1: offer and access

- Publish or privately share the diagnostic page.
- Ask for three introductions to people who personally own a recurring delivery report.
- Hold at least one artefact-led conversation using a sanitised report.
- Record the process and baseline in `prospect-scorecard.csv`.

## Week 2: proposal

- Complete up to three qualified conversations.
- Produce a fixed-scope proposal only where the decision, owner, data and consequence are real.
- Request payment or an explicit procurement step. Do not exchange speculative feature work for vague interest.

## Weeks 3–4: delivery or stop

- Deliver one paid diagnostic if accepted.
- Measure customer and internal effort.
- Capture the proceed, repeat or stop decision and reason.
- Build only what blocks the agreed diagnostic or protects its data.

## Decision at day 30

| Evidence | Decision |
|---|---|
| Paid diagnostic and accepted outcome | Improve the same journey and test repeatability |
| Strong problem and proposal rejected on scope/price | Revise the offer once using the recorded objection |
| Interest but no artefact, owner or budget route | Stop building and change acquisition approach or target segment |
| Existing tools solve the problem adequately | Stop this proposition or narrow to a different unmet decision |
| Diagnostic delivery consumes likely annual contract value | Sell it transparently as consulting or stop productising it |

Connector count, test count, page views and complimentary feedback do not pass this gate.
