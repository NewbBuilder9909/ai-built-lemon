# Interview script (30 minutes, no demo, show nothing)

**Who:** people who sign off progress or hours reported by suppliers they
don't control: client-side programme offices (UK and Welsh public sector
frameworks, SI-led programmes), PMO consultancies, prime contractors.

**Rules:** don't pitch, don't name the product, don't show a screen. Take
their words down verbatim.

1. Tell me about the last programme where several suppliers reported to you.
2. What do you sign off, and on what evidence?
3. The last time a supplier's figures turned out to be wrong: what happened?
4. What did it cost (money, time, relationship, a missed decision)?
5. How did you find out? Who checked, and how long did it take?
6. What tools do the suppliers use? What do you already use to check?
7. Have you tried an AI assistant on this? What happened?
8. What do you do about it today, and what does that cost per month?
9. If someone checked the files for you in ten working days, what would that
   be worth? (Let them name a number first.)
10. Who else would have to agree? Who holds the budget?
11. Who else should I talk to?

**Record per call:** date, role, organisation type, incident (yes/no, recent,
costly), their quote, budget holder, willing to discuss a paid diagnostic.

**Stop rule (direction.md Q-3/Q-4):** proceed only if at least two of five
describe a recent, costly incident and agree to discuss a paid diagnostic.
