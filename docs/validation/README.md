# Validation: prove it or stop

Three tools used in order. Nothing here needs code. Success is users and
money, not tests: two buyers describe a costly incident, and one pays for the
diagnostic.

1. [interview-script.md](interview-script.md): five conversations, no demo.
2. [rovo-benchmark-scorecard.md](rovo-benchmark-scorecard.md): what an in-tool assistant can and can't see.
3. [outreach-tracker.md](outreach-tracker.md): who was contacted, what they said.

The design for an AI-assistant connector is parked in
[../mcp-read-only-design.md](../mcp-read-only-design.md) until the pilot lands.

## Agents may help around the product

| Agent job | Rule |
|---|---|
| Sceptic | Before any build step, answer "who has asked for this?" and argue for stopping. |
| Interview synthesis | Summarise notes into a problem statement; quote the buyer's words, never paraphrase a claim into existence. |
| Outreach research | Public information only; 30–50 organisations; no personal data beyond a public job title. |
| Pack drafting | Narrative only. Numbers and citations come from the deterministic Evidence Check calculator. |
